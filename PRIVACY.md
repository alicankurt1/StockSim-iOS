# StockSim Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 October 2026_

StockSim is an educational paper-trading simulator. It is open source under the MIT license.

## Data we collect

**None.** StockSim does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

- No account or sign-in.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
- The TestFlight / release build makes no network requests. Prices are generated on the device by a random market simulator.

## Data stored on your device

Your virtual portfolio, watchlist and What If scenarios are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. They never leave the device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## TestFlight

If you install StockSim through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to send with the developer. This is handled by Apple under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). StockSim itself adds nothing to it.

## Not investment advice

All companies in the app are fictional and all prices are simulated. Nothing in StockSim is financial or investment advice.
